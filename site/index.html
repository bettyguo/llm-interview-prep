        How options are generated

        The correct answer is the first sentence of the entry's Short answer. The three distractors are bullets pulled from that entry's Common mistakes section — they're the exact failure modes interviewers listen for, so seeing them next to the right answer trains the discrimination you need.

        Entries with fewer than three documented mistakes are skipped in this mode.

            How this mode works

            You'll be shown questions one at a time on a countdown. Speak or jot your answer aloud as if in a loop, then reveal the model answer and rate yourself honestly (1–5). The final scorecard breaks down by topic and flags weak areas.

            Behavioral questions don't have a canonical answer — they show the signal the interviewer is listening for. Use that as the rubric.

            Mock interview

            Configure your round in the sidebar and hit Start interview. The timer runs per question. After each question, reveal the model answer and rate your performance — the final report shows your weakest topics for follow-up.
