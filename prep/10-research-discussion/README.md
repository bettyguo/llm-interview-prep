# 10 — Research Discussion & Paper Deep-Dives

For researcher and research-engineer roles, this segment is the entire bar. For ML-engineer roles, it's a probe — "tell me about a paper you read recently" — that distinguishes deep candidates from shallow ones.

## What you should walk in able to do

- Tell your own research / project story in **30 seconds**, **5 minutes**, and **30 minutes**.
- Read an unfamiliar paper in **15 minutes** and discuss it: contribution, method, evaluation, threats to validity, followups.
- Critique a paper without being either sycophantic or dismissive.
- Field "what paper did you read recently?" with a real answer that has a *take*, not a summary.
- Be literate in **bench suites**: HumanEval, MBPP, GSM8K, MATH, MMLU, MMLU-Pro, GPQA, BIG-Bench Hard, AGIEval, ARC-AGI, SWE-bench (Verified / Live), TAU-bench. Know what each measures and what each fails at.

## How to prepare

- Pick **3 papers** you can discuss in depth — one foundational (e.g. Attention Is All You Need), one recent (something from the last 6 months relevant to the role), one in your own track record.
- For each: write a **one-pager** with contribution / method / evaluation / threats / your-take. Verbalize until the 30-second version is automatic.
- Maintain a **read log** — papers + a sentence each — so "what have you read recently" never catches you flat.
