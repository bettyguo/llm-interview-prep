# ML & DL Fundamentals — aggregated references

## Textbooks

- [Bishop — *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning*](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/pattern-recognition-machine-learning/) — the canonical reference for fundamentals.
- [Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman — *The Elements of Statistical Learning*](https://hastie.su.domains/ElemStatLearn/) — statistical learning.
- [Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville — *Deep Learning*](https://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — DL canonical text.
- [Murphy — *Probabilistic Machine Learning*](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/book2.html) — modern probabilistic ML.

## Bias / variance / double descent

- [Belkin et al. — "Reconciling modern machine-learning practice and the classical bias–variance trade-off"](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1903070116) — double descent.
- [Nakkiran et al. — "Deep Double Descent"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.02292) — deep-net double descent.

## Optimization

- [Kingma & Ba — "Adam"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6980) — Adam.
- [Loshchilov & Hutter — "Decoupled Weight Decay Regularization" (AdamW)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05101) — AdamW.
- [Loshchilov & Hutter — "SGDR"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.03983) — cosine schedule.

## Architectures / normalization / regularization

- [He et al. — "Deep Residual Learning"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385) — ResNet.
- [He et al. — "Delving Deep into Rectifiers"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.01852) — Kaiming init.
- [Ioffe & Szegedy — "Batch Normalization"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167) — BatchNorm.
- [Ba, Kiros, Hinton — "Layer Normalization"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.06450) — LayerNorm.
- [Zhang & Sennrich — "RMSNorm"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07467) — RMSNorm.

## Loss functions

- [Lin et al. — "Focal Loss"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02002) — focal loss.

## GBM / tabular

- [Grinsztajn, Oyallon, Varoquaux — "Why do tree-based models still outperform deep learning on tabular data?"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08815) — GBM-vs-DNN tabular benchmark.
- [Chen & Guestrin — "XGBoost"](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02754) — XGBoost.

## Metrics / evaluation

- [Saito & Rehmsmeier — "Precision-Recall Plot... on Imbalanced Datasets"](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0118432) — PR-AUC.
- [Kaufman et al. — "Leakage in Data Mining"](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2382577.2382579) — data leakage taxonomy.
- [scikit-learn — cross-validation docs](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html) — practical CV.
