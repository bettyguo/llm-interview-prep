# 11 — Behavioral & Communication

The interview many technical candidates under-prepare for, and the reason offers fall through after a clean technical loop. The behavioral round is graded on *signal*: ownership, scope, impact, collaboration, judgment under ambiguity.

## What you should walk in able to do

- Tell **5–7 stories** from your work that, in aggregate, hit every common behavioral prompt: conflict, ambiguous spec, missed deadline, mentoring, hardest bug, biggest mistake, hardest decision, project you're proudest of.
- Tell each story in **STAR** form (Situation, Task, Action, Result) without bloat.
- Field **AI-specific behavioral**: a time you chose not to use ML; a time a model failed in production; how you debugged a hallucination; how you communicated model risk; how you decided between fine-tuning, RAG, and prompting.
- Show ownership — speak in first person about decisions you made, not the team passively.

## How interviewers grade

They're not memorizing your story. They're translating it into a rubric: scope of impact, ambiguity tolerance, collaboration, ownership, technical judgment. Your job is to make those signals visible. Each entry's `**Signal.**` block names the one or two signals the question is fishing for.
