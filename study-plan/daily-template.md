# Daily template + weekly review

The rhythm to use inside any plan.

## Daily (≈ 2.5 hr, scalable)

| Block | Time | What you do |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 1 | 60 min | Read one topic section. Annotate weak spots. |
| 2 | 45 min | Answer 3 representative questions **out loud**, self-graded against the worked answer. (Out loud matters — the gap between "I know it" and "I can say it" is huge.) |
| 3 | 45 min | One coding drill **or** one system-design drill (alternating across the week). |
| 4 | 15 min | Behavioral journaling: add to your STAR-story doc, even just one bullet. |

### Pacing notes

- If you have less time, drop block 3 first, then block 1. Never drop block 2 — speaking your answers is the irreplaceable part.
- If you have more time, double block 2 with a partner mock instead of solo.

## End of each day

- Note the **single question** you'd most like to re-do tomorrow morning.
- Note **one fact** you didn't know an hour ago.

## Weekly review (Sunday, ≈ 90 min)

1. **Re-take the topic self-quiz** for the week's topic.
2. **Re-answer 2 questions** you struggled with during the week, out loud, no notes.
3. **Update your your-own-work narrative.** New numbers, new scope, new lessons.
4. **Pick the focus question** for next week (e.g. "by end of next week I can explain MoE routing as fluently as I can explain attention").
5. **Schedule a mock** for the upcoming week. Skipping mocks is the most common failure mode.

## Mock interview hygiene

- Use a friend, a peer, a paid partner (Pramp, Interviewing.io), or a recording of yourself.
- After every mock: **write a one-pager** of what went well and what didn't. Re-read it before the next mock.
- Real interviews regress to the mean of your mocks. If your mocks are bad, your real interview will be bad. The fix is more mocks, not more reading.

## What to do the 24 hours before the interview

- **Sleep 8 hours.** Genuinely the highest-ROI prep activity in this window.
- Light review only — re-read your own behavioral one-pager and your top-3 paper one-pagers.
- Do **not** learn a new topic the night before.
- Pre-stage water, notepad, calm pre-interview routine.

## What to do the 24 hours after the interview

- Write **everything you remember being asked**, while it's fresh. Even questions you nailed.
- Add the questions to your study list. Future-you (or future-contributor-you) will thank you.
